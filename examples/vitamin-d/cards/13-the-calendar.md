São Paulo ran first

The calendar: São Paulo had enrolled all its patients before the Córdoba result appeared

São Paulo enrolled its first patient on 2 June 2020 and its last on 27 August 2020. The Córdoba result appeared on 29 August 2020. The São Paulo result was published on 17 February 2021.

Not ruled out: the Córdoba programme had been in the public registry since 29 April 2020. There is no evidence that the São Paulo team planned around it.
