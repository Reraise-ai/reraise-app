40–60 · up to 100 “perfectly safe”

Michael Holick: 40 to 60 ng/ml is a good range, up to 100 is perfectly safe

Michael Holick, MD, PhD, Boston University, in an interview published on 16 May 2018: “But because of the variability of the assays that are out there, 40-60 is a good range to be in.” And: “Up to 100 is perfectly safe.” He says a committee he chaired recommended a minimum of 30 ng/ml.

He gives 40–60 as a margin above a minimum of 30, because lab tests vary. Holick is on the GrassrootsHealth call’s list of endorsing scientists.
