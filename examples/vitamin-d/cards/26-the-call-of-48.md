GrassrootsHealth · 40–60

GrassrootsHealth: 48 researchers sign a call for 40 to 60 ng/ml

GrassrootsHealth, a US organisation founded by Carole Baggerly, runs the “Scientists’ Call to D*action”, begun more than fifteen years ago: the level for the general population “should be 40-60 ng/ml (100-150 nmol/L)”. According to its website, 48 senior vitamin D researchers have signed since.

A signed call, not a trial. William Grant, Michael Holick and Sunil Wimalawansa are on its list of endorsing scientists. In April 2026 GrassrootsHealth wrote that the range may extend to 40–80. It works with supplement suppliers, who may be its sponsors (declared in Grant’s 2020 paper).
