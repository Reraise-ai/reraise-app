open, no placebo

Córdoba was an open pilot trial without placebo

From the Córdoba paper itself: “This pilot study has several limitations as it is not double-blind placebo controlled.” Patients were assigned at random, but there was no placebo. The authors write that larger trials will be required to show a definitive answer. The vitamin D level of the patients was not measured.

This card sits on the refuting side, but its origin is the Córdoba trial.
