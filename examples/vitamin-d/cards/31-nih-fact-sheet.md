20 is enough · caution above 50

US National Institutes of Health: 20 ng/ml is sufficient for most people, possible harm above 50

The Office of Dietary Supplements of the US National Institutes of Health: “Levels of 50 nmol/L (20 ng/mL) or more are sufficient for most people.” Its table lists levels above 125 nmol/L (50 ng/ml) as “Linked to potential adverse effects, particularly at >150 nmol/L (>60 ng/mL)”.

For the statement on harm the fact sheet refers to the US Food and Nutrition Board. The page blocks automated reading. We hold an excerpt, not a full copy.
