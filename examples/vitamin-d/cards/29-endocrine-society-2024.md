Endocrine Society guideline, 2024: no clear evidence for a target level to prevent disease

A guideline panel reviewed the trials in people who have no medical reason for vitamin D treatment. It found “no clear evidence defining the optimal target level of 25(OH)D required for disease prevention” and suggests against routine testing. For healthy adults under 75 it suggests against taking more than the recommended daily intake to lower the risk of disease.

It does suggest extra vitamin D for children and adolescents, people over 75, pregnant women and people with high-risk prediabetes. It says further research is needed to determine optimal levels. We read the abstract only.
