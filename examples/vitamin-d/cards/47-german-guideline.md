Kluge and the German guideline group, 2021: a recommendation against vitamin D3 in hospital

Stefan Kluge and co-authors of the German guideline on hospital treatment of COVID-19, in a published reply in Deutsches Ärzteblatt (2021): the published randomised trials “showed no benefit for the administration of vitamin D3”, and “the guideline group articulated in the updated guideline a recommendation against vitamin D3”. That many severely ill patients have low levels does not, they write, make the low level the cause.

The only trial this reply cites is São Paulo. So this card and the São Paulo trial count as one origin. The guideline itself we have not read. Kluge declares research support and fees from a number of drug and medical-device companies.
