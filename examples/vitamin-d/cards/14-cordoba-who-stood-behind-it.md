public funds, the maker’s drug

Córdoba: a regional research programme, and the maker of the drug

The paper thanks a COVID research programme of the health ministry of Andalusia and a research foundation in Córdoba, and names the same two as funders of the larger trial that followed. It has no conflict-of-interest statement. The calcifediol was a Faes Farma product; Faes Farma holds the marketing authorisation for it. The registry of the larger trial names a public research institute as sponsor, and the government of Andalusia, Faes Farma and a second company, Dynamic Solutions, as collaborators.

Not shown: that Faes Farma paid for the trial, or whether the drug was donated or bought. The posts on its own site do not say. Who Dynamic Solutions is, we could not establish. The EU register entry belongs to a later trial that Faes Farma sponsored itself; we use it only to show who holds the authorisation.
