Brian Lamkin, Lamkin Clinic: the optimal level is 60 to 80 ng/ml

Brian Lamkin, DO, founder of the Lamkin Clinic in Edmond, Oklahoma, on his clinic’s website: “In functional medicine, the optimal 25-OH vitamin D level is 60 to 80 ng/mL.” He writes that the usual threshold of 30 ng/ml prevents bone disease but is not the level linked to optimal immune function. He pairs high-dose D3 with vitamin K2 and checks magnesium first.

Names no single study for the range. A table on the same page gives 60 to 100 ng/ml as the functional-medicine target. The page belongs to a clinic that offers the test and the treatment.
