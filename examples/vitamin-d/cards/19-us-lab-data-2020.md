US lab data, 2020: the higher the vitamin D level, the fewer positive coronavirus tests, up to about 55 ng/ml

Quest Diagnostics, a US laboratory company, matched the coronavirus tests of 191,779 people from March to June 2020 with vitamin D results it had measured for the same people in the year before. Positive tests: 12.5% below 20 ng/ml, 8.1% at 30–34 ng/ml, 5.9% at 55 ng/ml and above. Above 55 the rate did not fall further: 6.0% at 55–59, 5.9% above that.

A link in lab records, not a trial: people with high levels differ in other ways too. Four of the five authors are Quest employees and three own Quest stock. The fifth, Michael Holick, is a consultant to Quest. Quest paid their salaries and his fee and, according to the paper, had no other role in the study.
