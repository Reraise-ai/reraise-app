Calcifediol raises the vitamin D level faster than vitamin D3 (measurement, 2014)

Vitamin D3 is the precursor. The liver turns it into calcifediol, the form that is measured in blood. In a 2014 measurement in 35 healthy women, five per group, all who took calcifediol daily or weekly reached the target level, after 17 days on average. With vitamin D3, 70% did, after 68 days. After a single dose, calcifediol put more than twice as much into the blood in the first four days.

A 2018 review that says the same was written by two of the Córdoba authors.

Measurement, 2014: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24516879/

Review, 2018: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29713796/
