could chance explain it?

Two MIT researchers recalculate the Córdoba result (preprint, 2020)

Irwin Jungreis and Manolis Kellis took the published Córdoba numbers and asked: if the treatment did nothing, how often would chance alone produce a difference this large? Their answer: less than once in a million times, provided the patients were assigned at random. They call for immediate, well-designed trials of early calcifediol. A preprint, not peer-reviewed. The authors declare no funding and no competing interest.

Adds no new patients. It goes back to the same 76 people in Córdoba and counts as one origin together with the trial.
