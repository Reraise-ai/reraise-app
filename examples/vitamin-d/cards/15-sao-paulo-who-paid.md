São Paulo: two public research funders, no company

The paper names two funders. FAPESP is the research foundation of the state of São Paulo; by its own account its budget is 1% of the state’s tax revenue. CNPq is Brazil’s national research council, founded by law in 1951. The paper states that “the funders had no role in the design and conduct of the study”, nor in analysing or publishing it. No company appears in the paper or in the registry. The hospital pharmacy prepared the vitamin D3.

We read the paper, the registry and the funders’ own pages. We did not search the funders’ grant lists or other ties of the authors.
