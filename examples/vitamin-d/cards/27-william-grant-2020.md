William Grant and colleagues, 2020: raise the level above 40 to 60 ng/ml

William Grant and colleagues in the journal Nutrients (April 2020): people at risk of influenza or COVID-19 should consider 10,000 IU of vitamin D3 a day for a few weeks, then 5,000 IU. “The goal should be to raise 25(OH)D concentrations above 40-60 ng/mL (100-150 nmol/L).” They ask for trials to test this.

A recommendation, not a trial result. Four co-authors are from GrassrootsHealth. Grant declares funding from Bio-Tech Pharmacal. One co-author sells vitamin D supplements.
