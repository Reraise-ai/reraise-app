Córdoba pilot trial, 2020: calcifediol for patients in hospital with COVID-19

76 patients in the Reina Sofía hospital in Córdoba, Spain, assigned at random. 50 got calcifediol on the day of admission and again on days 3 and 7. 26 did not. Intensive care: 1 of 50 with calcifediol (2%), 13 of 26 without (50%).

PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32871238/

Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7456194/

A trial of treatment in hospital, not of prevention. The full case is on the field “Two trials, two answers”.
