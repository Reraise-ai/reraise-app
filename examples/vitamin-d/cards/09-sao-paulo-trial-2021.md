São Paulo trial, 2021: one high dose of vitamin D3 for patients in hospital with COVID-19

240 patients at two sites in São Paulo, Brazil. Half got one dose of 200,000 IU of vitamin D3, half a placebo. Double-blind. Days in hospital (median): 7.0 against 7.0. Intensive care: 16.0% against 21.2%, no reliable difference.

The paper and its reference list do not mention calcifediol or the Córdoba trial.

PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33595634/

Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7890452/

A trial of treatment in hospital, not of prevention: one high dose, given on average 10.3 days after symptoms began. The full case is on the field “Two trials, two answers”.
