Sunil Wimalawansa: keep the level between 50 and 80 ng/ml

Sunil Wimalawansa, MD, PhD, in a GrassrootsHealth text carried by the site eLanka (last updated 11 Nov 2023): the level “needs to be ‘maintained’ between 50 to 80 ng/mL range; this would cover approximately 99.9% of all human disorders”. In a 2022 review he writes that immune cells need a level “above 50 ng/mL”.

The page names GrassrootsHealth as its source, and he is on the call’s list of endorsing scientists. So this card belongs to that circle. “99.9%” is his claim, not a trial result. In the 2022 review he declares no conflict of interest.
