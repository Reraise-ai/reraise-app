zero deaths at 50, in theory

Borsche, Glauner and von Mendel, 2021: a calculated point of zero COVID-19 deaths at 50 ng/ml

Three authors took published data from one population study and seven clinical studies and drew a line through them: the higher the vitamin D level, the lower the COVID-19 death rate. Extended, the line reaches zero at about 50 ng/ml: “Regression suggested a theoretical point of zero mortality at approximately 50 ng/mL D3.” They recommend raising levels above 50 ng/ml, “despite ongoing vaccinations”.

The paper calls itself a systematic review and meta-analysis. Its authors call the zero point theoretical and write that the data “provide strong evidence that low D3 is a predictor rather than just a side effect of the infection”. Taken separately, the two sets of data miss the usual threshold for statistical significance (p = 0.077 and 0.065). Pooled, they meet it (p = 0.019). Two of the three authors are independent researchers. They declare no competing interests. We read the abstract only.
