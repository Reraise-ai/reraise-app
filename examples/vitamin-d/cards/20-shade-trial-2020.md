SHADE trial, 2020: high-dose vitamin D3 for people with a positive test and a low level

A randomised, placebo-controlled trial in India. 40 people with a positive test, no or mild symptoms and a vitamin D level below 20 ng/ml. 16 got 60,000 IU of vitamin D3 a day for 7 days, aiming for a level above 50 ng/ml. 24 got a placebo. Virus no longer detectable within three weeks: 10 of 16 (62.5%) against 5 of 24 (20.8%).

Small. It measured whether the virus was still detectable, not illness or death. The authors declare no competing interests. We read the abstract only.
