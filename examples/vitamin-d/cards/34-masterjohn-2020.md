30–40 ng/ml

Chris Masterjohn: keep the level in the 30 to 40 ng/ml range

Chris Masterjohn, PhD, in an update to his own article on 13 Sep 2020: “I believe the best thing to do is maintain 25(OH)D in the 30-40 ng/mL range”. In the same update he writes that he had been wrong to expect vitamin D to raise the risk in COVID-19 and that, for treating the illness, “The treatment effect of vitamin D has been established”. He adds that he has seen nothing to overturn his concern about ACE2, the receptor the virus uses.
