16 studies · fewer cases

Sartini et al., 2024: a meta-analysis of vitamin D taken before infection

Sartini and colleagues pooled 16 publications on vitamin D taken as prevention. “Our findings indicate that vitamin D supplementation has a protective effect against the incidence of COVID-19 in RCT studies (OR 0.403, 95% IC 0.218, 0.747) …”. They also report fewer admissions to intensive care.

In the randomised trials the odds of infection were about 60% lower (odds ratio 0.40; the 95% confidence interval runs from 0.22 to 0.75). The CORONAVIT trial, which found no reduction, is in the reference list. Whether it is among the 16 is not clear from the abstract. The authors declare no conflicts of interest.
