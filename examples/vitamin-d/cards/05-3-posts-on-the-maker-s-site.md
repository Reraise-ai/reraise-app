Faes Farma: three posts about the Córdoba result on the company’s website (2020)

Faes Farma sells the calcifediol capsules used in Córdoba. Its website carried the Córdoba result three times in 2020: a press release on 8 Sep, a report that ends with a paragraph about the product on 10 Sep, and a report from a symposium it co-sponsored on 3 Dec. Two of the three quote the trial’s senior author.
