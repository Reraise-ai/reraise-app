European Food Safety Authority, 2016: target level 50 nmol/L (20 ng/ml)

The European Food Safety Authority set an adequate intake of 15 µg (600 IU) a day for adults, chosen so that “most of the population will achieve a serum 25(OH)D concentration near or above the target of 50 nmol/L”. That is 20 ng/ml.

EFSA opinion: https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4547
