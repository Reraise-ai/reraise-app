10.3 days after symptoms

São Paulo gave the dose on average 10.3 days after symptoms began

From the São Paulo paper itself: the mean time from the onset of symptoms to randomisation was 10.3 days. The authors write that the dose came after a relatively long time, name this as a limitation, and add that further studies should test early vitamin D3. The level was measured on the day of the dose and at discharge, not in between.

This card sits on the supporting side, but its origin is the São Paulo trial.
