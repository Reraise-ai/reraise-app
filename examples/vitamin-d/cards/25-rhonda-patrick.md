Rhonda Patrick: the sweet spot is between 40 and 60 ng/ml

Rhonda Patrick, PhD, in an episode posted on 6 Mar 2024: “it seems as though the sweet spot for vitamin D is between 40 and 60 nanograms per milliliter.” She points to analyses of deaths from all causes in studies up to about 2015.

She names 60 as the upper end of her range, not the middle.
