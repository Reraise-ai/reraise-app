Cochrane review, 2021: not enough evidence for vitamin D as a treatment of COVID-19

A Cochrane review of vitamin D as a treatment for COVID-19 (24 May 2021): “There is currently insufficient evidence to determine the benefits and harms of vitamin D supplementation as a treatment of COVID-19.”

About treating people who are ill, not about prevention. The review rests on three trials: Córdoba, SHADE and São Paulo. All three are on this board, two of them on the supporting side. It adds a judgement, not new patients. State of 2021. We have not checked for a newer version.
