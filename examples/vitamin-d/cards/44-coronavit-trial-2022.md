CORONAVIT trial, 2022: testing for vitamin D and supplementing did not reduce COVID-19

6,200 people aged 16 and over in the United Kingdom. Half were offered a blood test and, if their level was below 30 ng/ml (75 nmol/L), 800 or 3,200 IU of vitamin D a day for six months. The other half were offered nothing. No placebo. Result: this “was not associated with a reduction in risk of all cause acute respiratory tract infection or covid-19”.

The doses are below the 4,000 IU Nehls names. Whether participants reached 50 ng/ml we have not read. The authors declare funding from, among others, makers of vitamin D supplements. Whether that money went into this trial the record does not say. We read the abstract only.
