German Nutrition Society (DGE): a level of at least 50 nmol/l (20 ng/ml)

The German Nutrition Society (DGE) names a blood level of at least 50 nmol/l (20 ng/ml) as desirable. It estimates that 20 µg (800 IU) a day are needed when the body makes no vitamin D of its own.

Reference values (German): https://www.dge.de/wissenschaft/referenzwerte/vitamin-d/
