# Tutorials

Tutorials are learning-oriented lessons: you follow them step by step and end up with
something that works.

**Status: none exist yet.** The Worldbuilder is in milestone M0 (contracts and tooling) and
there is no runnable product to learn on. A tutorial that cannot be followed would be a
claim without proof, so none is published.

## Planned

| Tutorial | Planned for | What you will do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your first Lage | M1 | Start the board, open a Lage from the synthetic Hormuz world, place a card, draw a `supports` edge, accept an agent's proposal and see the state survive a reload. |
| Connect your vault in five minutes | M1/M2+ | Point the board at a Markdown vault or a second brain through a storage adapter. |
| Seat an agent at your board | M1 | Connect an MCP client, read a Lage and make a proposal. |

While you wait, the [Hormuz scenario](../product/scenarios.md) shows in prose what the first
tutorial will do, and the [roadmap](../roadmap.md) says when.
