> “The German adult general population shows urinary nickel concentrations below 3 μg/l. This value can therefore be used as a reference value.”

Quoted from Umweltbundesamt (UBA), Nickelgehalte in Nahrungsmitteln, Trinkwasser und Luft, Evaluierung eines Referenzwertes für die Nickelkonzentration im Harn.

- context: Historical overview (as of before 2011) of nickel content in foods, drinking water and air. Gastrointestinal absorption is below 15%. Urinary nickel >3 µg/L indicates raised oral intake. Faecal excretion ~260 µg/day.
- bloodTarget: Nickel in urine: <3 µg/L (reference value for adult non-smokers in Germany)
