> These results of this study indicate a no-observed-adverse-effect-level (NOAEL) value for L-threonine to be 12 g/day in healthy adult males.

Quoted from Matsumoto et al., Evaluation of safe utilization of l-threonine for supplementation in healthy adults: a randomized double blind controlled trial.

- context: Safety study on supplementation in healthy adult men over 4 weeks.
- form: L-threonine capsules (Ajinomoto), 3–12 g/day in 1–3 single doses
- upperLimit: NOAEL 12 g/day; no serious adverse events
- bloodTarget: Plasma L-threonine and L-2-aminobutyrate rose significantly at 6, 9 and 12 g/day
- perDay: NOAEL: 12 g/day (4 weeks, healthy adult men)
