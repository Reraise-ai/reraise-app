> After the age of 12 years, patients with PKU should aim for blood Phe levels of 120–600 μmol/L. … The evidence for patients <12 years of age is strong indicating that a Phe concentration of 360 μmol/l should be considered as the upper target Phe concentration.

Quoted from van Wegberg et al. (European PKU Guidelines Group), The complete European guidelines on phenylketonuria: diagnosis and treatment, Recommendations / Section on target blood Phe levels.
