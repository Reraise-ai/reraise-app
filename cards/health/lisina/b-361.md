> [...] the separate derivation of recommended intake values for the individual essential amino acids is waived. For adults under 65 years, a recommended intake of 0.8 g/kg body weight per day is derived from this.

- context: The DGE does not recommend an isolated lysine intake but covers the amino acid requirement through total protein intake.
- perDay: No separate reference value for lysine; requirement is covered via protein intake of 0.8 g/kg body weight/day (adults under 65)
