> The Panel endorses the Average Requirement (AR) for adults (men and women) of 1.3 mg NE/MJ (about 5.5 mg NE/1 000 kcal) and the Population Reference Intake (PRI) of 1.6 mg NE/MJ (about 6.6 mg NE/1 000 kcal) adopted by the SCF (1993).

Quoted from EFSA, Scientific Opinion on Dietary Reference Values for niacin, Abstract und Summary.

- context: Dietary Reference Values for the European population
- form: Niacin equivalent (NE); separate ULs for nicotinic acid and nicotinamide
- upperLimit: UL nicotinic acid: 10 mg/day (adults); UL nicotinamide: 900 mg/day (adults)
- perDay: 1.6 mg NE/MJ (about 6.6 mg NE/1000 kcal) for adults; equals about 13–15 mg NE/day at 2000 kcal
