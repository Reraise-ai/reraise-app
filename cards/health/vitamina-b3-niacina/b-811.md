> The Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR) recommends a maximum amount of 160 milligrams (mg) per recommended daily portion per product for adding nicotinamide to food supplements.

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Höchstmengenvorschläge für Niacin in Lebensmitteln inklusive Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln, Abschnitt 1.1, Tabelle 1.

- context: Proposed maximum amounts for food supplements and fortified foods
- form: Separate maximum amounts for the three niacin sources (nicotinamide, nicotinic acid, inositol hexanicotinate)
- upperLimit: Nicotinamide: 160 mg/day in supplements; nicotinic acid: 4 mg/day in supplements; inositol hexanicotinate: 4.4 mg/day in supplements
