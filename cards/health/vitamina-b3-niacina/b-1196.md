> The Panel endorses the Average Requirement (AR) for adults (men and women) of 1.3 mg NE/MJ (about 5.5 mg NE/1 000 kcal) and the Population Reference Intake (PRI) of 1.6 mg NE/MJ (about 6.6 mg NE/1 000 kcal) adopted by the SCF (1993).

Quoted from EFSA, Scientific Opinion on Dietary Reference Values for niacin, Chapter 4.1 Adults; SCF 2002 UL Opinion.

- context: All age groups; ULs apply to supplements, not to food
- form: Separate ULs for nicotinic acid (flushing) and nicotinamide (NOAEL-based)
- upperLimit: UL: nicotinic acid 10 mg/day; nicotinamide 900 mg/day (SCF 2002)
- perDay: PRI 1.6 mg NE/MJ (approx. 13–19 mg NE/day depending on energy requirement)
