> The recommended intake for women from 19 years with low, medium and high phytate intake is 7 mg, 8 mg and 10 mg zinc per day respectively; for men the values are 11 mg, 14 mg and 16 mg zinc per day.

Quoted from DGE (D-A-CH-Referenzwerte), DGE-FAQ Zink 2019 / D-A-CH-Referenzwerte für die Nährstoffzufuhr, Frage 3 'Wie hoch sind die Referenzwerte für die empfohlene Zufuhr von Zink?'.

- context: Germany/Austria/Switzerland; reference values for nutrient intake
- form: Phytate-dependent reference values; zinc from whole grains/legumes is less available
- upperLimit: UL 25 mg/day for adults (via SCF/EFSA)
- perDay: 7–10 mg (women), 11–16 mg (men), depending on phytate intake (low/medium/high)
