> No Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL) has been derived for biotin … The evaluation of the available data showed sufficient evidence for possible interactions of oral biotin intakes of 150 µg per day and above with clinical laboratory tests

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Höchstmengenvorschläge für Biotin in Lebensmitteln inklusive Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln, Abschnitt 2.3 „Höchstmengen für Biotin“.

- context: The BfR recommends a warning notice on food supplements because of laboratory test interference
- upperLimit: No UL; but risk of laboratory test interference from 150 µg/day
