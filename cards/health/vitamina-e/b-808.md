> The Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR) recommends a maximum amount of 30 milligrams (mg) of vitamin E per recommended daily portion of a food supplement

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Höchstmengenvorschläge für Vitamin E in Lebensmitteln inklusive Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln, Tabelle 1, Abschnitt 2.3.1.

- context: Proposed maximum amounts for food supplements; warning for men from 55 about prostate cancer risk with uncontrolled supplementation
- form: Tocopherol-rich extracts of natural origin (E306), alpha-tocopherol (E307)
- upperLimit: 30 mg per recommended daily portion (food supplement); 60 mg total maximum
