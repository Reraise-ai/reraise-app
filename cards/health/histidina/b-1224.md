> For infants from 6 months and for children, adolescents and adults, the values for the requirement of indispensable amino acids published by the World Health Organization (WHO) in 2007 are adopted.

Quoted from DGE (D-A-CH-Referenzwerte), DGE-FAQ: Protein und unentbehrlichen Aminosäuren.

- context: The DGE gives no separate reference value for histidine, because the requirement is considered met within the general protein intake (0.8 g/kg/day) on a balanced diet.
- perDay: No separate value; refers to WHO/FAO (2007)
