> Healthy adults (over 18 years): 3000–4000 mg of EPA+DHA per day. [...] supplemental intake of up to 5000 mg per day is usually well tolerated

Quoted from Nelba Villagrán, Dosis de Omega-3: ¿Cuánto EPA y DHA debo tomar?, Abschnitte „Recomendaciones de dosificación“ und Sicherheit.

- context: Practical dosage recommendation from orthomolecular medicine; deviates clearly from official recommendations (3–16x higher)
- form: Concentrated EPA+DHA products; blood test to determine the omega-3 index recommended
- upperLimit: Up to 5,000 mg/day are described as safe
- perDay: Adults: 3,000–4,000 mg EPA+DHA/day; children: 2,000 mg; adolescents: 2,000–3,000 mg
