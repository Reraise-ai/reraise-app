> The Panel concludes that silicon is bioavailable from choline-stabilised orthosilicic acid and that its use in supplements, at the proposed use levels of the source, is of no safety concern

Quoted from EFSA, EFSA Journal 2009 — Choline-stabilised orthosilicic acid added for nutritional purposes, Scientific Opinion.

- context: In 2009 EFSA classified ch-OSA as a safe silicon source for supplements. The typical intake (20-50 mg/day) is considered harmless. OSA is the best absorbable form.
- form: Choline-stabilized orthosilicic acid (ch-OSA) — highest bioavailability; MMST as alternative
- perDay: 5-10 mg Si/day (ch-OSA)
