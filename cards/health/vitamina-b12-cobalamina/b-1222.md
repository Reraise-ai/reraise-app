> There is consistent evidence in adults that a cobalamin intake of 4 µg/day and above is associated with serum concentrations of holoTC and cobalamin within the reference ranges derived from healthy subjects, together with MMA and tHcy concentrations below proposed cut-off values in adults, which indicates an adequate cobalamin status.

Quoted from EFSA, Scientific Opinion on Dietary Reference Values for cobalamin (vitamin B12), Abstract und Abschnitt 5.3.
