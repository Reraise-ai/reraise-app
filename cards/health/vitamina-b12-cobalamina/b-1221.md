> The Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR) recommends a maximum amount of 25 micrograms (µg) of vitamin B12 per recommended daily portion of a food supplement.

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Höchstmengenvorschläge für Vitamin B12 in Lebensmitteln inklusive Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln, Tabelle 1, Abschnitt 2.3.1.

- context: Proposed maximum amounts for food supplements to ensure safe intake
- upperLimit: 25 µg/day from food supplements; 6 µg/100 g in fortified foods; 1.6 µg/100 ml in drinks
- perDay: 25 µg per recommended daily portion of a food supplement (maximum amount)
