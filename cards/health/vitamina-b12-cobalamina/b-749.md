> The Panel sets an Adequate Intake (AI) for cobalamin at 4 µg/day for adults based on the data on different biomarkers of cobalamin status.

Quoted from EFSA, Scientific Opinion on Dietary Reference Values for cobalamin (vitamin B12), Abstract, S. 6-7.

- context: AI for adults based on biomarkers (serum cobalamin, holo-TC, MMA, tHcy); pregnant women 4,5 µg, breastfeeding women 5 µg
- upperLimit: No UL defined (no demonstrable harmful effects)
- bloodTarget: Serum cobalamin, Holo-TC, MMA and tHcy as biomarkers; AI based on 4 µg/day with adequate biomarkers
- perDay: 4 µg (adults, AI)
