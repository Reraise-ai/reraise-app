> I only recommend supplementing with copper in the case of fixing a verified deficiency. In any other case, 1 mg per day is a reasonable hedge against deficiency from unknown variation within foods, but I would not supplement with more than that.

Quoted from Chris Masterjohn, Why You Should Manage Your Copper Status and How to Do It.

- context: Adults with suspected or proven copper deficiency; Masterjohn advises prevention, not high doses
- form: Copper sulfate or copper glycinate (Solgar); together with zinc and vitamin C
- perDay: 1 mg/day as a precaution; 2–3 mg 2–3× per week in case of proven deficiency
