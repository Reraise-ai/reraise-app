> No, the Federal Institute for Risk Assessment does not in principle recommend taking more than 1 milligram of copper per day in the form of food supplements. Children and adolescents should not get any extra copper at all.

Quoted from Verbraucherzentrale, Kupfer zur Nahrungsergänzung?, FAQ, Stand: 01. Dezember 2025.

- context: Recommendation based on BfR and EFSA; warns against high doses (3 mg/day)
- upperLimit: max. 1 mg/day from supplements
- perDay: 1–1.5 mg (sufficient supply from food)
