> The Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR) recommends a maximum amount of zero for copper in food supplements (NEM) that are also intended for adolescents from 15. For supplements aimed at adults, the BfR recommends a maximum of 1 mg per recommended daily portion of a supplement, with product labelling stating that the product is not suitable for children and adolescents.

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Höchstmengenvorschläge für Kupfer in Lebensmitteln inklusive Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln, Tabelle 1: Höchstmengen; Tabelle 2: Zufuhrreferenzwerte und UL.

- context: Proposed maximum amounts for food supplements; adolescents from 15 have no room for additions, since the baseline intake already reaches/exceeds the UL
- upperLimit: UL 5 mg/day (adults, based on SCF 2003); UL 4 mg/day (adolescents from 15)
- perDay: 1 mg per recommended daily portion (supplements for adults)
