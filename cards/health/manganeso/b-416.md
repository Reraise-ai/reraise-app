> The recommended daily minimum intake for adults from age 10 is 2–5 mg/d (D-A-CH reference values); in the USA 1.9–2.3 mg/d is recommended from age 9; during breastfeeding 2.6 mg/d. The maximum daily dose without toxic side effects is 6 mg/d from age 9, 11 mg/d from age 18 (USA FNIC).

Quoted from Johann Josef Kleber, Mangan — Naturmedikation.de, Abschnitt Dosierung.
