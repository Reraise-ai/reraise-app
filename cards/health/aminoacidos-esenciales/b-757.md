> The daily intake recommendations stated for adults range from 4-5 mg per kg body weight for tryptophan up to 39-42 mg per kg body weight for leucine. The amino acid requirement is met through daily protein intake (for adults 0.8 g per kg body weight).

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Gesundheitliche Bewertung von Aminosäuren, Abschnitt „Aminosäurenbedarf“.
