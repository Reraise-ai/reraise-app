> With higher intakes of BCAAs in isolated form there are indications of possible health risks. These concern in particular undesirable changes in laboratory parameters, for example raised blood ammonia levels.

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Nahrungsergänzungsmittel - Isolierte verzweigtkettige Aminosäuren können bei hoher Aufnahme die Gesundheit beeinträchtigen, Stellungnahme Nr. 052/2019.

- context: For adults; applies to isolated intake in addition to the usual diet; not for children, adolescents, pregnant or breastfeeding women or persons with impaired kidney function
- form: Isolated branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs); the BfR recommends taking BCAAs not individually but in combination
- upperLimit: Guidance values for isolated BCAA intake (in addition to food): leucine 4.0 g/day, isoleucine 2.2 g/day, valine 2.0 g/day, BCAAs total 8.2 g/day
