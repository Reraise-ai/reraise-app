> Daily supplementation with compositions of all the EAAs in free form has been shown to be beneficial in many ways. Mainly, compositions of EAAs in free form stimulate protein synthesis and whole-body protein turnover.

Quoted from International Society of Sports Nutrition (ISSN), International Society of Sports Nutrition Position Stand: Effects of essential amino acid supplementation on exercise and performance, ISSN Position Stand (J Int Soc Sports Nutr, 2023).

- context: For athletes and older people to maintain muscle mass
- form: Free EAA isolates (without protein) absorbed faster than whole proteins; higher blood concentrations
- perDay: 1,5–18 g EAA (free form) for muscle protein synthesis; 3 g EAA ≈ 20 g whey protein
