> Strictly speaking, there is no physiological requirement for protein, but for the nine indispensable amino acids and for the element nitrogen. The updated reference values for protein intake therefore also give the average requirement of indispensable amino acids in mg/kg body weight per day in a table.

Quoted from DGE (D-A-CH-Referenzwerte), FAQ Protein und unentbehrlichen Aminosäuren.

- context: For healthy adults; reference values for nutrient intake
- form: The EAA requirement is met through protein intake; no separate EAA reference values, since amino acids are usually supplied as proteins
- perDay: 0.8 g protein/kg body weight/day (adults up to 65); 1.0 g/kg/day (from 65)
