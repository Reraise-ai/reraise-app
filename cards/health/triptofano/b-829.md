> For people who do not do regular endurance sport, giving 0.5 g to 1.5 g tryptophan in the evening on an empty stomach, with the greatest possible distance before and after meals, would be advisable.

Quoted from Jörg Carls, Tryptophan – Einsatz in der Komplementärmedizin.

- context: Carls recommends taking tryptophan on an empty stomach to avoid competition with other amino acids.
- form: On an empty stomach, 3 h after and 2 h before meals; cofactors: vitamin B6 (1.5 mg), magnesium (375 mg), folic acid (200 µg), omega-3 (1.5 g)
- perDay: 0.5–1.5 g in the evening on an empty stomach; with endurance sport 1.5 g 30 min before exertion
