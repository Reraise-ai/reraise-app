> The human requirement for sulfur is covered by a sufficient supply of sulfur-containing amino acids (cystine, cysteine, methionine). Sulfur is therefore not dealt with separately.

Quoted from DGE (D-A-CH-Referenzwerte), Positionspapier der Deutschen Gesellschaft für Ernährung e.V. — DGE-FAQ Referenzwerte allgemein.

- context: The DGE gives no separate reference values for sulfur, since with sufficient protein intake enough sulfur is normally taken in.
- form: Sulfur as a component of insulin, sulfatides and keratin; intake via sulfur-containing amino acids
- perDay: no separate reference value; the requirement is covered by sulfur-containing amino acids (cystine, cysteine, methionine)
