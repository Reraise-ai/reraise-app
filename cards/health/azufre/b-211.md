> Sulfate requirements are thus met when intakes include recommended levels of sulfur amino acids. For this reason, neither an Estimated Average Requirement (and thus a Recommended Dietary Allowance) nor an Adequate Intake for sulfate is established.

Quoted from National Academies (IOM), Dietary Reference Intakes for Water, Potassium, Sodium, Chloride, and Sulfate — Chapter 7: Sulfate, Kapitel 7, Seiten 424–448.

- context: For the general population; sulfate requirement is covered by the recommended intake of sulfur-containing amino acids
- form: Inorganic sulfate (SO4²⁻) is needed for PAPS synthesis; main source is breakdown of methionine/cysteine
- upperLimit: EPA recommends 250 mg/L sulfate in drinking water (taste/tolerability); WHO 400 mg/L
- perDay: No RDA/AI for sulfate; requirement covered by sulfur-containing amino acids
