> Sulfate requirements are thus met when intakes include recommended levels of sulfur amino acids. For this reason, neither an Estimated Average Requirement (and thus a Recommended Dietary Allowance) nor an Adequate Intake for sulfate is established.

Quoted from National Academies (IOM), Dietary Reference Intakes for Water, Potassium, Sodium, Chloride, and Sulfate, Chapter 7: Sulfate.

- context: The sulfate requirement is covered by the recommended intake of sulfur-containing amino acids; neither EAR nor AI set for sulfate.
- form: Inorganic sulfate (SO4²⁻) for PAPS synthesis; main source is the breakdown of methionine and cysteine
- upperLimit: no UL set for sulfate
- perDay: no EAR/RDA/AI for sulfate; the requirement is covered by sulfur-containing amino acids (methionine, cysteine)
