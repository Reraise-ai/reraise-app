> Sulfate requirements are thus met when intakes include recommended levels of sulfur amino acids. For this reason, neither an Estimated Average Requirement (and thus a Recommended Dietary Allowance) nor an Adequate Intake for sulfate is established.

Quoted from National Academies (IOM), Dietary Reference Intakes for Water, Potassium, Sodium, Chloride, and Sulfate — Kapitel 7: Sulfate, Kapitel 7, Abschnitt 'Sulfate Requirement'.

- context: Adults and children — total requirement is considered sufficiently covered by a protein-rich diet
- form: Organic sulfur via methionine, cysteine, cystine, taurine, glutathione, thiamine (B1) and biotin (B7/B8)
- upperLimit: No UL set for sulfate (insufficient data); EPA recommends 250 mg/L sulfate in drinking water
- perDay: No RDA set for sulfate/sulfur; requirement is covered by the sulfur amino acids (methionine + cysteine)
