> An isolated sulfur deficiency is, however, not known according to the German Nutrition Society.

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), MSM - hilft die Schwefeltherapie bei Arthrose? (Verbraucherzentrale, zitiert BfR/DGE).

- context: BfR and DGE confirm: an isolated sulfur deficiency is not known. MSM is not a remedy but a food supplement with no proof of effect.
- form: MSM (methylsulfonylmethane) as an organic sulfur compound
- upperLimit: no maximum amount for sulfur/MSM; MSM is classed as a novel food, no maximum or minimum amounts at national or European level
