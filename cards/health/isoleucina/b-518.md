> There is so far no general recommendation by the German Nutrition Society (DGE) on the daily intake of isoleucine. The daily requirement of an adult is probably 19 mg per kilogram body weight. [...] You should take at most 2.2 g of isoleucine per day via supplements, recommends the Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR).

Quoted from MITOcare (Mikronährstofflexikon), Enciclopedia de micronutrientes de isoleucina — MITOcare, Abschnitte „Wie viel Isoleucin brauchst du am Tag?“ und „Überdosierung“.

- form: L-isoleucine; component of proteins

- perDay: ~19 mg/kg body weight/day (estimated)
