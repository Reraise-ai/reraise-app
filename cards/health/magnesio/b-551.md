> For oral magnesium replacement, a dose between 240 and 1000 mg of elemental magnesium divided into 1 to 3 doses is recommended. For patients unable to take oral intake or with magnesium concentrations below 1.5 mg/dl, intravenous supply is used.

Quoted from Hospital Británico de Buenos Aires, Guía de práctica clínica: uso de magnesio en internación.

- context: Inpatients with hypomagnesaemia; individual adjustment required
- form: Sulfate (i.v.), oral replacement in 1–3 single doses
- upperLimit: >12 mg/dl (life-threatening, respiratory depression)
- bloodTarget: 1.7-2.2 mg/dl (normal), <1.5 mg/dl (hypomagnesemia)
- perDay: 240–1000 mg (oral replacement), 400 mg/day (basic requirement)
