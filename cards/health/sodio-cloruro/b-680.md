> The DGE gives a guideline value of 6 g per day for table salt. In its recently revised estimates for adequate intake, however, it recommends 1.5 g sodium and 2.3 g chloride daily, which converted into table salt would be 3.8 g.

Quoted from L.M. Jacob, DGE optimisiert Referenzwerte für Kalium, Natrium und Chlorid, Artikel auf drjacobsweg.eu.

- context: General population; argues for a low-salt, potassium-rich diet instead of strict salt restriction
