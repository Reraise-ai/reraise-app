> They were given 10 g of pantothenic acid a day in 4 divided doses. To enhance the effect, the patients were also asked to apply a cream consisting of 20% by weight of pantothenic acid to the affected area, 4-6 times a day.

Quoted from Lit-Hung Leung, Pantothenic acid deficiency as the pathogenesis of acne vulgaris (Medical Hypotheses 1995), Medical Hypotheses, Vol. 44, Issue 6, S. 490–492.

- context: Open-label study without control group, 100 patients (1995)
- form: Calcium D-pantothenate orally + 20% pantothenate cream topically
- perDay: 10 g/day (acne treatment), divided into 4 single doses
