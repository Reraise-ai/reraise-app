> EFSA has classed boron as a non-essential nutrient. So far no specific physiological or biochemical function of the mineral could be identified in humans or higher animals. Recommended intakes are given neither by EFSA (EFSA, 2004) nor by the D-A-CH societies (D-A-CH, 2019).

Quoted from EFSA, Höchstmengenvorschläge für Bor in Lebensmitteln inklusive Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln (BfR zitiert EFSA), Kapitel 2.1, Tabelle 2.

- context: EFSA classes boron as a non-essential nutrient; no recommended intakes, since no specific physiological function in humans is proven
- form: Boric acid and sodium borate assessed as boron sources
- upperLimit: UL = 10 mg/day for adults (2004); reassessment 2013: group ADI = 0.16 mg/kg body weight/day
