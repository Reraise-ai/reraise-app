> For adults, a remaining amount of 1 mg per day is available for supplementation. It is recommended to allocate this amount to food supplements alone [...] Given the further gaps in knowledge about boron (BfR, 2006), an uncertainty factor of 2 is applied to derive a maximum amount for supplements, which gives a maximum of 0.5 mg per recommended daily portion of a food supplement for adults.

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Höchstmengenvorschläge für Bor in Lebensmitteln inklusive Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln, Kapitel 2.3.1, Tabelle 1.

- context: For adults; supplements with boron should carry the notice 'Not suitable for children and adolescents', because children and adolescents reach or exceed the UL even without supplements
- form: Boric acid (E 284), sodium borate/sodium tetraborate (E 285), calcium fructoborate
- upperLimit: 0.5 mg from supplements (remaining 1 mg ÷ uncertainty factor 2); EFSA UL = 10 mg/day from all sources
- perDay: 0.5 mg per recommended daily portion (food supplement)
