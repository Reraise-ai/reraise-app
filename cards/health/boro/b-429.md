> The WHO classifies a daily boron intake of 1–13 mg as an 'acceptable safety interval' for adults. According to EFSA, adults should not take in more than 10 mg boron per day. [BfR names] a maximum amount of 0.5 mg boron per daily consumption recommendation for adults (BfR, 2021).

Quoted from Labor Dr. Bayer, Das Ultraspurenelement Bor: Ein Booster für Vitamin D und Knochen?, Abschnitt „Bor in der Nahrung, Boraufnahme, Grenzwerte“.

- upperLimit: WHO: 1–13 mg as 'acceptable safety interval'; EFSA UL: 10 mg/day; BfR maximum for supplements: 0.5 mg/day
- perDay: 3 mg boron/day (to support bone health, after Rondanelli et al. 2022)
- bloodTarget: Serum approx. 20–100 μg/l
- context: Adjuvant use in degenerative and inflammatory skeletal diseases; the evidence is assessed critically (many studies >20 years old, small cohorts, heterogeneous designs)
