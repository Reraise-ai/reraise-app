> DOSAGE: no recommendations for the daily minimum intake from the DGE and USA FNIC known; the maximum daily dose without toxic side effects from age 18 is up to 20 mg/d (FNIC USA). OPTIMAL SUPPLEMENTATION DOSE: 6–9 mg/d

Quoted from Johann Josef Kleber, Bor – Naturheilkundliche Krebstherapie (Dr. Kleber), Einleitung / Dosierung.

- context: Orthomolecular medicine; recommended in osteoporosis because it reduces renal calcium and magnesium excretion; also discussed in prostate cancer and cognitive function
- upperLimit: 20 mg/d (FNIC USA)
- perDay: 6–9 mg/day (optimal supplementation dose); max. 20 mg/d without toxic side effects from age 18
