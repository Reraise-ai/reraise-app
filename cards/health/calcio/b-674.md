> Calcium intake by tablets of more than 500 milligrams per day can lead to kidney stones and vascular calcification. The German Society for Endocrinology (DGE) points this out. Since many patients take several food supplements, accidental overdosing is also possible, the professional society says. For calcium-rich foods, however, there is no harmful upper limit.

Quoted from DGEndo (Deutsche Gesellschaft für Endokrinologie), Endokrinologen warnen vor Komplikationen durch Kalziumpräparate.

- context: Warning about complications from calcium preparations, Ärzteblatt 2022
- form: Calcium intake preferably from food; with osteoporosis drugs (bisphosphonates, teriparatide, romosozumab) additional supplementation if needed
- upperLimit: Up to 500 mg from supplements is harmless; above 500 mg/day it can cause kidney stones and vascular calcification
- perDay: 1,000 mg/day optimal
