> The Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR) recommends a maximum amount of 500 milligrams (mg) per recommended daily portion for adding calcium to food supplements. The total daily intake of calcium from food supplements should not exceed 500 mg per day.

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Höchstmengenvorschläge für Calcium in Lebensmitteln inklusive Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln, Tabelle 1.

- context: Proposed maximum amounts for calcium in foods including food supplements
- form: For additions of 250 mg calcium or more per daily portion: advice to avoid other calcium-containing supplements
- upperLimit: 500 mg per day from supplements; UL 2,500 mg/day (EFSA)
- perDay: 500 mg from food supplements (maximum amount)
