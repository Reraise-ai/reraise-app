> No, it is not enough for the bones, for example, simply to measure or take calcium. The serum calcium usually tested is nowhere near enough to know about calcium metabolism. And yes, calcium alone is not enough. Calcium is not calcium. There are many forms of calcium compounds and by no means all of them work.

Quoted from Andreas Oberhofer, Orthomolekulare Therapie – Dr. Oberhofer.
