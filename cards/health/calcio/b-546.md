> Adults over 35 should have blood calcium levels in the 9's. Adults over 40 are not allowed to have calcium levels in the 10's. (rarely is ok, but not frequently, and not over 10.2)

Quoted from Jim Norman, Normal Blood Calcium Levels Adjusted for Patient's Age, Regeln für normale Blutcalciumspiegel.

- context: Individual blood calcium target values by age; laboratory normal ranges are too imprecise and should not be used for the diagnosis of hyperparathyroidism
- upperLimit: Serum calcium above 10.1 mg/dl in adults over 40 y. is not normal (individual age ranges)
- bloodTarget: Calcium in serum: adults over 35 y. should be between 9.4 and 9.9 mg/dl; over 40 y. not above 10.1 mg/dl; 9.7 mg/dl is the most frequent value (mode)
