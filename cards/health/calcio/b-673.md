> For osteoporosis patients without a specific drug therapy for osteoporosis, an intake of 1,000 mg calcium daily with food is recommended as basic therapy. Calcium supplementation should be carried out only if the recommended calcium intake is not reached with food. High single doses are not recommended here.

Quoted from DVO (Deutsche Vereinigung für Osteologie), DVO Leitlinie Osteoporose 2023 – 9. Therapie, 9.2.2 Calcium.
