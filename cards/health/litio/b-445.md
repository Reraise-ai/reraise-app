> The Federal Office of Consumer Protection and Food Safety (BVL) points out that the mineral lithium is not approved for use in food supplements in the European Union. It does not matter which chemical compound of lithium is used. This includes, for example, lithium carbonate or lithium orotate.

Quoted from BVL (Bundesamt für Verbraucherschutz und Lebensmittelsicherheit), Einnahme von Lithium nur unter ärztlicher Aufsicht.

- context: Lithium is not approved for use in food supplements in the EU; food supplements containing lithium are prohibited.
