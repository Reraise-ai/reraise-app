> The content of vitamins and minerals in dietary supplements may not exceed, in daily consumption, the Tolerable Upper Limits (UL) in TABLE I of this article.

Quoted from ANMAT – Código Alimentario Argentino, Código Alimentario Argentino — Artículo 1381 (Resolución Conjunta 3/2020), Artículo 1381, TABLA I (MINERALES).

- context: Argentina does not regulate lithium as a nutrient in supplements; neither IDR nor UL are set
- upperLimit: Lithium is not included in the UL table (TABLA I, Art. 1381) of the Código Alimentario Argentino — no official upper limit exists for lithium in dietary supplements
