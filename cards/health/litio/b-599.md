> The Reference Daily Intake (IDR) is defined as the level of daily intake that is sufficient to meet the nutrient requirements of almost all individuals of a group.

Quoted from ANMAT – Código Alimentario Argentino, Resolución 118/2008 — Ingesta Diaria Recomendada de Vitaminas y Minerales, Tabla I: Ingesta Diaria Recomendada de Proteína, Vitaminas y Minerales para Adultos.

- context: The IDR tables for vitamins and minerals (Art. 1387) contain no lithium entry — no official daily requirement defined
