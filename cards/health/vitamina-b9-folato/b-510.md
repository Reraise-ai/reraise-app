> A meta-analysis published in 2025 that pooled 45 randomised trials and 96,962 participants found a 15% reduction in the risk of stroke (RR 0.85; 95% CI 0.76–0.96) and a modest 5% reduction in overall cardiovascular disease (RR 0.95; 95% CI 0.90–0.99).

Quoted from Jorge Rojas, Ácido fólico: para qué sirve, dosis, efectos secundarios y precio (2026), Artículo completo.

- context: Rojas summarises the evidence: folic acid lowers homocysteine by ~25%, but no reduction of myocardial infarction or cardiovascular mortality; possible benefit for stroke in populations without fortification
- form: Synthetic folic acid; 5-MTHF as alternative in MTHFR polymorphisms; check B12 before high dose
- upperLimit: 1.000 mcg/day (UL for synthetic folic acid)
- perDay: 400–800 mcg/day (periconceptional); 4.000 mcg/day (high risk, only by medical prescription)
