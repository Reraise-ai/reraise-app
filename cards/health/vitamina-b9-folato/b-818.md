> For adults, the AR is determined from the folate intake required to maintain functional folate adequacy characterised by serum and red blood cell folate concentrations above 10 and 340 nmol/L, respectively. An AR of 250 µg dietary folate equivalents (DFE)/day and a PRI of 330 µg DFE/day are derived.

Quoted from EFSA, Scientific Opinion on Dietary Reference Values for folate.
