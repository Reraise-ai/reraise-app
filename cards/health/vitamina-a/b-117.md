> We recommend taking a multivitamin/mineral supplement that provides no more than 750 μg RAE of preformed vitamin A (usually labeled vitamin A acetate or vitamin A palmitate) and no more than 750 μg RAE of additional vitamin A as β-carotene.

Quoted from Linus Pauling Institute (Oregon State University), Vitamin A — Linus Pauling Institute, Oregon State University, Recommendation section.

- context: Healthy adults; LPI advises multivitamins with a maximum of 750 mcg RAE preformed vitamin A
- form: Retinol (as acetate or palmitate); beta-carotene as safe alternative
- upperLimit: 1.500 mcg RAE/day from retinol associated with lower bone health in older adults
- perDay: Maximum 750 mcg RAE preformed vitamin A in the multivitamin; 750 mcg RAE additionally as beta-carotene
