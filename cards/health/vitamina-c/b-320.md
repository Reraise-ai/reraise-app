> To get around this hurdle somewhat, there is vitamin C in liposomal form, which is absorbed much better. At 500 mg about 90% is absorbed. It is therefore also the best and best-tolerated way to take vitamin C. E.g. 2x 500 mg.

Quoted from Kai Grimme, Vitamin-C und Hochdosis Vitamin-C Infusionen, Artikel.
