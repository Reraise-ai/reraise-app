> No UL has been derived for vitamin B1, and no adverse health effects were observed even at high intakes. Given the very low toxicity of vitamin B1, the BfR sees no need for now to set a maximum amount for this vitamin, either for food supplements or for the fortification of foods for general consumption.

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Höchstmengen für Vitamin B1, Vitamin B2 und Pantothensäure in Lebensmitteln inklusive Nahrungsergänzungsmitteln, Kapitel 2.1.3.
