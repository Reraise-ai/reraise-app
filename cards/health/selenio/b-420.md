> For male adolescents from 15 and for adults the reference value for selenium intake is 70 µg per day, for female adolescents and adults 60 µg per day.

Quoted from DGE (D-A-CH-Referenzwerte), DGE-FAQ Selen / Referenzwerte für die Selenzufuhr, Tabelle Referenzwerte für die Selenzufuhr.

- context: Reference values (estimates of an adequate intake), derived from saturation of the plasma concentration of selenoprotein P (SePP)
- upperLimit: no upper limit of its own; refers to the EFSA UL
- perDay: 60 µg (women from 15), 70 µg (men from 15); breastfeeding women 75 µg
