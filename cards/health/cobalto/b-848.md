> The Federal Institute for Risk Assessment (BfR) recommends that food supplements for adolescents from 15 and adults should contain at most 25 micrograms (μg) of vitamin B12 per daily dose.

Quoted from BfR (Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung), Vitamin B12 – bei pflanzenbasierter Ernährung besonders auf eine angemessene Versorgung achten, Seite 3.

- context: The BfR recommends maximum amounts for supplements based on EFSA ULs. There is no maximum amount for cobalt itself, only for vitamin B12.
- form: Cobalamin (vitamin B12); hydroxocobalamin or cyanocobalamin
- upperLimit: Maximum amount of vitamin B12 in food supplements: 25 µg/day
