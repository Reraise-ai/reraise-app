> Art 156 - (Res 1546/85) In foods in general... the presence of the following metallic and non-metallic elements is tolerated within the limits set out below: ... Boron 80 ...

Quoted from ANMAT – Código Alimentario Argentino, Código Alimentario Argentino, Capítulo 3 — Contaminantes, Art. 156 — Límites máximos de elementos metálicos.

- context: The CAA regulates cobalt as a contaminant in food, not as a nutrient with daily requirement
- form: Cobalt as trace element/contaminant, not as a nutrient with an intake value
- upperLimit: Cobalt in foods: max. 80 mg/kg (Bor as comparison value in Art. 156); specific cobalt maximum values depending on food category
- perDay: No separate cobalt daily amount; cobalt only as contaminant with maximum values in foods (mg/kg)
