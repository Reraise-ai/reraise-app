> With repeated intake of cobalt salts in large amounts (20 to 500 mg per day) over a period of weeks, changes in the blood count such as raised haemoglobin values, an underactive thyroid and worsening of sight and hearing can occur.

Quoted from LGL Bayern (Bayerisches Landesamt für Gesundheit und Lebensmittelsicherheit), Lebensmittel: Kobalt.

- context: The LGL warns against high doses of cobalt salts, which can cause changes in the blood count, an underactive thyroid and worsening of sight and hearing.
- form: Cobalt salts in various compounds
- upperLimit: Toxicity at 20–500 mg/day (oral intake over weeks)
- perDay: Usual intake: 10–13 µg/day from food
