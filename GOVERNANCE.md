# Governance

Reraise is maintained by the maintainers of the Reraise-ai organisation (`@Reraise-ai/maintainers`), who act as
benevolent dictators for life (BDFL): the maintainers make final decisions on scope,
architecture, licensing and releases.

- Decisions of lasting consequence are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
- Anyone may propose changes through issues (feature, bug, ADR) and pull requests.
- Maintainers may be added by the existing maintainers; they get review and merge rights.
- Contributions require DCO sign-off, and for the AGPL-3.0-or-later directories a signed CLA; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Conduct is governed by [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

This model may evolve as the community grows; changes will be announced via ADR.
